Our Story

710 Detail Co. started as a real dream — not a side hustle, not a quick idea, but a commitment to building something meaningful from the ground up. A self‑run future built on hard work, loyalty, and the determination to create a better life.

Growing up around people who made their own way, Austin learned early that independence isn’t just a choice — it’s survival. The drive of self‑made creators, artists, and entrepreneurs lit the spark that eventually became 710 Detail Co. Their loyalty to their people and their refusal to quit shaped the foundation of this business.

This company was built to give family and friends a place to work hard, grow, and succeed — not for some random employer, but for themselves. A place where effort actually matters, where people are lifted up instead of used up, and where second chances are part of the mission.

Austin and his wife, Kelsi, put in the work to get things started — learning, investing, practicing, and proving their shine one job at a time. With the support of their close friend Tieler, who’s rebuilding his life with intelligence, grit, and real motivation, the team is creating something strong, honest, and built to last.
