About 710 Detail Co.

Built from the ground up with loyalty, hard work, and a dream worth chasing.

Our Story

710 Detail Co. started as a dream — a real one. A dream to build something meaningful, self‑run, and built from the ground up through hard work, loyalty, and determination. Not a job. Not a side hustle. A future.

Growing up around people who carved their own paths, I learned early that independence isn’t just a choice — it’s a lifestyle. Stories of self‑made creators, artists, and entrepreneurs lit a fire in me. Their drive, their loyalty to their people, and their commitment to building something of their own inspired me to do the same.

I wanted to create something that gave my family and friends a place to work hard, succeed, and grow — not for some random employer, but for ourselves. Something that could make a real difference in our lives and the lives of the people around us.

Together, my wife Kelsi and I put in the work to get our foot in the game — learning, investing, practicing, and proving our shine one job at a time. And now, with the help of a lifelong friend who shares the same vision, we’re building something strong, honest, and built to last.

710 Detail Co. is more than detailing.
It’s independence.
It’s loyalty.
It’s family.
It’s the grind.
It’s the belief that we can make anything happen — and we’re doing exactly that.

Built With Heart

We believe in loyalty, hard work, and showing up for the people who show up for us. 710 Detail Co. was built with that energy — the kind that comes from real life, real struggle, and real inspiration.

Every detail we do carries a little bit of that spark. Not loud. Not flashy. Just real.
